Company Overview
Orion Health Systems (OHS) is a mid-sized healthcare software provider founded in 2012.
The company builds clinical workflow tools used by hospitals and outpatient clinics.
OHS operates primarily in the United States, with secondary customers in Canada.

Core Products
OHS has three primary products: PulseEMR, CareTrack, and InsightAI.
PulseEMR is an electronic medical record system designed for small to mid-sized hospitals.
CareTrack is a patient engagement and appointment scheduling platform.
InsightAI provides analytics dashboards for hospital administrators.

PulseEMR Details
PulseEMR supports HL7 and FHIR standards for interoperability.
The system includes modules for prescriptions, lab results, and clinical notes.
PulseEMR does not currently support pediatric growth chart visualization.

CareTrack Details
CareTrack sends automated reminders via SMS and email.
It integrates with PulseEMR but can also operate as a standalone product.
CareTrack reduced missed appointments by 18 percent in pilot studies.

InsightAI Details
InsightAI uses historical hospital data to identify operational bottlenecks.
The product does not provide real-time clinical decision support.
InsightAI dashboards are refreshed every 24 hours.

Security and Compliance
All OHS products are HIPAA compliant.
Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.
Data in transit is protected using TLS 1.2 or higher.
OHS does not store patient data outside North America.

Pricing and Licensing
PulseEMR is licensed on a per-bed, per-year pricing model.
CareTrack pricing is based on the number of active patients.
InsightAI is sold as an annual enterprise subscription.
OHS offers discounts for non-profit hospitals.
