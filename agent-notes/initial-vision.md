# IA School App Vision

## Purpose
Build a school-first platform for IA that centralizes announcements, club operations, events, calendar visibility, and Flex Friday workflows for students and staff.

## What Success Looks Like
- Students and staff can reliably discover what matters today from one dashboard.
- Clubs run in a structured way with invite-only access and sponsor-governed approvals.
- School and club events appear in one calendar feed with meaningful filters.
- Flex Friday session assignment and registration are clearly managed.
- Every important action is auditable.

## Primary User Groups
- Students
- Student Leaders
- Student Admins
- Teachers
- Sponsors
- Support Staff
- Admins
- Super Admins

## Core Experience Pillars
- Clear permissions and accountability by role.
- Announcement-first communication over chat-heavy behavior.
- Predictable event publishing and approval lifecycle.
- Mobile-friendly access with web parity.
- High trust through reporting, keyword controls, and immutable audit trails.

## Out of Scope Direction
- No 1:1 student DMs.
- No parent/guardian user accounts.
- No multi-school tenancy in the current product direction.
