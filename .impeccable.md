## Design Context

### Users
Innovation Academy students, teachers, sponsors, and school admins use this app to keep up with announcements, events, schedules, clubs, and school-day logistics. The interface needs to work quickly during an active school day, often for users checking one thing at a time between classes.

### Brand Personality
Clear, capable, school-centered. The product should feel organized and dependable, with enough warmth and polish to avoid looking like internal admin software or a generic dashboard template.

### Aesthetic Direction
Stay within the current IA product language: light-first, clean surfaces, rounded panels, restrained accent color, and readable density. Favor practical hierarchy over decorative effects. Interfaces should feel modern and calm, not flashy, futuristic, or gaming-themed.

### Design Principles
1. Prioritize what matters right now: current class, unread announcements, upcoming events, and today’s calendar context should surface first.
2. Keep school workflows legible: labels, timestamps, statuses, and scopes should be obvious without extra clicks.
3. Use polish, not spectacle: subtle contrast, spacing, and layout rhythm should do most of the work.
4. Make state explicit: read/unread, archived/active, club scope, and schedule context should always be visible.
5. Preserve consistency across student-facing and staff-facing views so the product feels like one system.
