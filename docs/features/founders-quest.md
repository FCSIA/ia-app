# Founders’ Quest recognition

Both halls use muted, theme-aware metal and wood accents. Hall of Finders
retains its wooden plaque layout; Hall of Founders retains its card deck.

The annual Finders plaque lists the first 30 student quest completions in
completion order. Years follow America/New_York calendar boundaries (January 1),
not school years. Earlier years remain accessible through the year controls.
Completion records are never reset when a year changes. Each student’s first
quest completion determines their plaque year; completion after place 30 still
unlocks both halls but does not add a name to that year’s plaque. Inactive
accounts retain their recognition. Names use the current preferred display name;
deleted accounts are omitted rather than exposing a retained private name.

Discovery guide for maintainers (contains spoilers):
- Copper: seven consecutive taps on the Profile What’s new version, followed
  by opening the temporary seal.
- Jade: after Copper, find March 2 in Calendar and tap its egg five times.
- Crystal: after Jade, find the small traveler on Day Progress and clear five
  gates. Unlimited retries and widening after three misses remain available.
- Bonus secrets: type phoenixphoenix outside input fields; tap Calendar Today
  seven times; tap the Fun Fact heading seven times. These grant the bonus marker,
  not an alternative hall unlock.

Tap sequences reset after a 1.5-second gap. Keyboard sequences reset after a
five-second gap or an ignored key. Claims remain playful client-triggered
recognition, not a tamper-proof competition. Header keys still disappear two
minutes after completion; saved progress and hall access remain.
