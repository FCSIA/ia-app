## Spring 2027 Events & Pathway Details

_Source: `_IA Spring Events 2027 NEW..pdf`_

### Pathway Flex Fridays

- **Engineering Flex Fridays** (`1/29`, `2/21`[^feb-date])
  - Designed to give students hands-on, real-world learning experiences connecting to Engineering.
  - Topics include: ENG process design, hand tools, SolidWorks, 3D printing, pneumatics, welding, and more.
- **Health Science Flex Fridays** (`2/5`, `3/12`)
  - Designed to give students hands-on, real-world learning experiences connecting to Health Science.
  - Topics include: patient care, nutrition, EMR, Biotechnology, Surgical Tech, and more.
- **IT Flex Fridays** (`2/26`, `3/26`, `4/16`)
  - Designed to give students hands-on, real-world learning experiences connecting to Information Technology.
  - Topics include: web development, programming, networking, AI, cryptology, game design, and more.

### Special Spring Events

- **Making Fun! Engineering Event** — `3/5` (`9:00 AM – 3:00 PM`, come any time)
  - Hands-on, imagination-fueled event where high school engineering students bring wild ideas to life inspired by Elementary students. Seeking volunteers with expertise in design, fabrication, and engineering to support students in building their inventions.
- **Save-A-Life** — `4/23` (Drill starts at `1:00 PM`)
  - IA's largest hands-on healthcare simulation returns for its third year. Students step into real-world emergency, clinical, and leadership roles as they respond to a large-scale tornado disaster.
- **Showcase** — `5/20` (Thursday)
  - Students present their year-long projects, highlighting creativity, innovation, unique solutions, and original ideas. _(Note on flyer: awaiting final graduation date update in the spring.)_

### Business Partner Opportunities on Flex Fridays

On any given Flex Friday, Business Partners are invited to help with:

- Running a 2-hour workshop on skills relating to their field from either `10:15 – 12:15` or `2:00 – 4:00` (or both)
- Giving a TED-talk-style seminar
- Teaching soft skills needed for the real world
- Presenting a real-world problem-solving challenge

[^feb-date]: `_IA Spring Events 2027 NEW..pdf` lists `2.21` for the February Engineering Flex Friday, whereas `2026-2027 Flex Friday Schedule NEW.pdf` lists Friday `2.12`.
